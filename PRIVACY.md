# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Cadence is a timetable and daily planner that runs entirely on your device.

## What Cadence collects

Nothing. Cadence has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.
It does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

## What Cadence stores

Your timetables, tasks, attendance records and settings are saved in a single
file (`cadence.json`) in the app's private data folder on your device, with one
backup copy of the previous version. This data leaves your device only when you
choose to export, back up or share it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**, **alarms & reminders** and **full-screen alarms** — to
  remind you before classes, activities and tasks, and to ring alarms you set.
- **Do Not Disturb access** (optional, Android) — to silence your phone during
  activities you mark that way.
- **Run at startup** (Android: after a reboot; desktop: at sign-in, if enabled)
  — so reminders and widgets keep working.

None of these are used to collect information.
